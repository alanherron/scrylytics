# Scrylytics Branding and Website Planning Notes

A complete reference document containing the creative, conceptual, and technical planning for the **Scrylytics** project — including brand identity, elemental system, gemstones, visual metaphors, website layout, and cinematic worldbuilding.

---

## 🌌 Overview

Scrylytics is an AI-powered analytics and storytelling ecosystem for Hearthstone players, designed as a mystical, data-driven world composed of **five Halls**, each representing a distinct form of insight and mastery.

---

## 🏛 The Five Halls of Scrylytics

Each Hall embodies one of the five classical elements and serves as a symbolic domain of knowledge within the Scrylytics universe.

## 🏔️ Hall Descriptions & Environments

### 🌍 Decklytics — The Foundation of Earth
A grand stone hall filled with runic monoliths, scrolls, and glowing data engravings.  
Mood: Grounded, wise, scholarly.  
Lighting: Golden beams through emerald skylights.  
Function: Analyze decks, structure, and synergy.  
> “Stone remembers every hand that shaped it.”

### 🌬️ Playlytics — The Breath of Air
A skyborne observatory of glass and wind, with data streams drifting in the air.  
Mood: Light, kinetic, analytical.  
Lighting: Bright refracted hues of silver and cyan.  
Function: Visualize play flow, tempo, and timing.  
> “In motion lies truth — the pattern between thought and action.”

### 🔥 The Forge — The Flame of Fire
A vast dwarven forge powered by molten lava, brass conduits, and holographic flame data.  
Mood: Energetic, experimental, volcanic.  
Lighting: Dynamic ember and brass tones.  
Function: Simulate and refine decks through data-forging.  
> “Creation demands fire — only through the crucible is mastery forged.”

### 💧 Metalytics — The Tides of Water
A cathedral of reflection where data flows like rivers of light through transparent walls.  
Mood: Calm, introspective, adaptive.  
Lighting: Soft sapphire glow with mirrored surfaces.  
Function: Analyze meta trends, archetype evolution, and adaptation.  
> “Water reflects all that stands above it — if you can still your surface long enough to see.”

### ✨ The Oracle — The Mind of Aether
A suspended, zero-gravity sphere of light and runes, the center of the Scrylytics nexus.  
Mood: Enlightened, serene, infinite.  
Lighting: Prismatic, soft, divine.  
Function: AI foresight — interprets all other halls.  
> “All paths lead to the center, where understanding becomes illumination.”

## 🧭 Website Planning & UX Design

### Homepage (The Nexus)
- Central **crystal orb** divided into five glowing segments — each one a gateway to a Hall.  
- Hover state: Hall illuminates with its elemental color.  
- CTA: “Enter the Hall of Insight.”  
- Filters: Toggle overlays to show data activity or elemental balance.

### Decklytics Landing View
- Hero Section: “The Foundation of Strategy” → CTA: Analyze a Deck  
- Composition Panel: Upload deck or enter code; instant visualization of mana curve and card types.  
- Synergy Map: Interactive network of related cards.  
- Insights Feed: Generated textual insights and meta comparison.  
- Footer: Cross-navigation to The Forge.

### The Forge Landing View
- Hero Image: Dwarven hammer with a glowing triangular ruby inset — resting atop a hot anvil.  
- Background: Lava crucibles pouring molten metal; steam and sparks.  
- Focus: Gem glows with radiant heat; forge flickers behind.  
- CTA: “Begin Forging.”

### The Oracle Page
- Hero: Floating opal sphere surrounded by golden runes.  
- Function: Unified AI insight; query prompt interface.  
- Interaction: Natural-language search and visualization.

## 🎨 Visual Style & Interaction Notes

Color Palette:  
- Backgrounds → Charcoal (#0B0B10) or Marble (#F7F7FB) depending on theme.  
- Accents → Elemental hues per hall.  
- Highlight Lines → Mana Gold (#E3C37C).

Typography:  
- *Spectral* for headers and taglines.  
- *Montserrat* for UI and body text.

Motion & Interaction:  
- Hover glow and smooth 300ms ease transitions.  
- Elemental color shifts when interacting with hall filters.  
- Parallax mountain visuals for Decklytics hero.  
- Animated ember particles for The Forge.

Layout:  
- 12-column grid, max width 1280px.  
- 32px section padding, 16px internal gutters.  
- Fixed nav with blurred glass background and active highlight.

## 🔥 Forge Cinematic Scene (Hero Reference)
A photorealistic dwarven hammer inspired by Thor’s Mjölnir resting on a glowing black anvil.  
The hammer’s head features **ornamental gold filigree** and a **large triangular ruby** inset on its side, glowing like molten fire.  
The background is a sprawling forge hall with **lava pouring from crucibles**, **steam clouds**, and **chains hanging in silhouette**.  
The camera focuses tightly on the gemstone while the forge blurs behind it, sparks flying through the air.

Mood: Reverent, mythic, powerful — the heart of the Forge made visible.

---

## 🏔️ Decklytics Visual Metaphor (Hero Scene)
A panoramic mountain range with five towering peaks, each representing an archetype or deck identity.  
When filters are applied (by class, meta rank, etc.), the mountains shift color and shape dynamically — reflecting the filter state.  
Clicking a peak reveals insights, deck stats, and an “Enter the Hall” launch action.  
Style: Stylized realism; emerald lighting and runic engravings.  
Mood: Scholarly and adventurous — data visualization meets mythic geography.

## 🧠 Summary
Scrylytics fuses **data visualization, fantasy storytelling, and AI interpretation** into a cohesive ecosystem.  
Each Hall reflects a way of knowing — structure (Earth), motion (Air), transformation (Fire), reflection (Water), and consciousness (Aether).  
The world is designed to feel immersive yet usable — a sacred interface for mastering both cards and understanding.
